HamOS SD card
=============

Copy everything in this folder to the root of a FAT32 microSD card, so that
the card holds a folder called HamOS. Then insert the card into the
Cardputer and switch it on.

HamOS/guide      Guide pages (text files; you can add your own)
HamOS/flags      Country flags
HamOS/map        Offline map data
HamOS/sstv       Pictures for SSTV (received pictures are saved here too)

HamOS writes its settings (config.txt), the logbook (log.adi) and other
files into HamOS as well.
