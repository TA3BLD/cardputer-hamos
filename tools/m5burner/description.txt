HamOS - amateur radio toolkit for the Cardputer / Cardputer ADV (LoRa + GNSS cap optional)

FT8 / FT4 / WSPR, SSTV, PSK31, CW and RTTY decoders, APRS (internet, LoRa-APRS 433.775 MHz, audio modem),
Meshtastic-compatible mesh with the Meshtastic phone app over Bluetooth, offline map, logbook with
callbook lookup and online log upload, POTA/SOTA spots, DX cluster, satellites with Doppler, radio
control (CI-V, Yaesu, Kenwood, IC-705 Bluetooth, rigctld), CW and voice keyers, emergency mode (SOS),
PC control panel over USB and WiFi. Turkish and English.

Copy the "sdcard" folder of the repository to a FAT32 microSD card (maps, flags, guide pages).
